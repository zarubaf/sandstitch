# S0409: connection cycle

A connection between bits that other connections already join closes a cycle in the connection graph. Repeating a connection exactly (the same two ends and ranges, in either order) is ignored and not reported; any other connection between joined bits, such as a connection of a sub-range of two ends already connected whole, or the third side of a triangle of inouts, is an error. Connections are taken in the order they were made, so the connection reported is the one that closed the cycle, with its ends in the order of the call. `ModDef::check` reports it once per component, about the component's first end in declaration order, with that connection as the primary label and the three newest connections of the component made before it as secondary labels (a note counts the rest); `emit` panics with it.

## Example

```rust
let top = ModDef::new("top");
let i = top.add_port("i", IO::Input(8));
let o = top.add_port("o", IO::Output(8));
o.connect(&i);
o.slice(3, 0).connect(&i.slice(3, 0));
let diagnostics = top.check();
```

```text
error[S0409]: connecting `top.o[3:0]` and `top.i[3:0]` closes a cycle
 --> src/main.rs:5:15
  |
4 | o.connect(&i);
  |   ------- connected here
5 | o.slice(3, 0).connect(&i.slice(3, 0));
  |               ^^^^^^^ connected here
  |
  = note: they are already connected through other connections
```

## Fix

Remove the redundant connection: the bits are already connected.
